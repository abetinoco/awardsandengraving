Good questions

Before you ask.

The things people most often want to know before their first order. Anything else — call, email, or just walk in.

How fast can you turn an order around?

Most single items are ready in about 3–5 business days, and we’ll always give you a firm date when we quote. Have a banquet or event that can’t slip? Tell us the deadline and we’ll tell you honestly whether we can hit it — rush work is often possible.

Can I bring in my own item to engrave?

Absolutely — it’s some of our favorite work. Gloves, tumblers, tools, glassware, knives, instruments. Bring it by the shop and we’ll tell you on the spot whether it will take the engraving well and what it’ll cost.

Do you have minimum order quantities?

No minimums. One pet tag gets the same care as three hundred team medals.

Will I see a proof before you engrave?

Yes — you approve a proof of the exact layout and wording before anything is cut. Nothing is engraved until you’ve signed off.

How does pricing work?

Every job is quoted individually based on the item, material, quantity and complexity of the engraving. Send us the details and we’ll reply with a price and timeline, usually the same business day.

What artwork or logo files do you need?

Vector files (AI, EPS, SVG or vector PDF) give the cleanest engraving. A high-resolution PNG or JPG usually works too — send what you have and we’ll tell you if we need anything better.

Do you handle team and league orders?

Every season. We’ve done Vernon Hills High School’s athletic awards for over twenty years and Libertyville Little League’s trophies for over fifteen. Send your roster and we’ll take it from there — names spelled right, ready for banquet night.

Do you ship orders?

Yes — team, school and corporate orders ship anywhere in the country. Local orders can be picked up at the shop at 332 N Milwaukee Ave.
