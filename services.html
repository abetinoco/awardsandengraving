How it works

From idea to engraved.

Custom doesn’t have to mean complicated. Most orders take four steps and less than a week.

Step i

Tell us the idea

Call, email, or walk in with the occasion, the item, and rough wording. Not sure yet? Describe it and we’ll help you land it.

Step ii

Quote & proof

We price the job — usually the same business day — and you approve a proof of the exact layout before anything is cut.

Step iii

Made in-house

Engraved right here on Milwaukee Avenue. Nothing is farmed out, so nothing gets lost in translation.

Step iv

Pick up or ship

Walk in and pick it up downtown, or we’ll ship team and corporate orders anywhere in the country.

Our shop

Eight machines, so the material never decides for you.

Laser engraving, rotary and diamond drag, laser marking and full-color UV printing — every one of those is a machine standing in our building on Milwaukee Avenue, not a promise we send out to somebody else. Two CO₂ lasers, two fiber markers for bare metal, a UV flatbed for real brand color, a New Hermes rotary and diamond-drag engraver, a sublimation press for full-color work, and a rotary station for bottles and stemware.
