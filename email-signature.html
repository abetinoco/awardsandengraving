Paste it in

Paste with Cmd+V or Ctrl+V. "Paste and match style" strips the layout.

Gmail

  1. Open the gear icon, then See all settings.
  2. On the General tab, scroll to Signature and choose Create new.
  3. Name it, click into the box and paste.
  4. Under Signature defaults, pick it for new emails and replies.
  5. Scroll to the bottom and choose Save Changes.

Outlook on the web and the new Outlook for Windows

  1. Open the gear icon, then Account and Signatures.
  2. Choose New signature, name it and paste.
  3. Pick it for new messages and replies, then Save.

Outlook for Mac

  1. In the menu bar, choose Outlook, then Settings and Signatures.
  2. Add one with +, select everything in the box and paste over it.
  3. Click another signature and back to check it saved, then set it under Choose default signature.

Classic Outlook for Windows

  1. Choose File, Options, Mail, then Signatures.
  2. Choose New, name it and paste into the edit box.
  3. Set it for new messages and replies, then OK.

If the logo shows as a blank square in a new message, the mail app is holding back remote images. It appears for the person who receives the email.
