Our story

A landmark, in caring new hands.

For over 77 years, Awards & Engraving has been a cornerstone of Libertyville’s historic downtown. In 2025, Daniel & Bailey Mattson took the reins — keeping the craft, the machines and the corner exactly where they belong.

Our niche expertise extends well beyond engraving. From trophies and memorial plaques to monogrammed gloves and one-off keepsakes, we handcraft tailored solutions for your unique vision.
