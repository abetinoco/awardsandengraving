Our story

A Libertyville landmark, in caring new hands.

For over 76 years, Awards & Engraving has been a cornerstone of Libertyville’s historic downtown. In 2025, Daniel & Bailey Mattson took the reins — keeping the craft, the machines and the corner exactly where they belong, with fresh energy behind them.

Our niche expertise extends well beyond engraving. From trophies and memorial plaques to monogrammed gloves and one-off keepsakes, we handcraft tailored solutions for your unique vision.
