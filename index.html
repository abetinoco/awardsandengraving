Our story

Libertyville's trophy shop since 1949.

For more than seventy-five years, Awards & Engraving has been part of downtown Libertyville — the shop locals have trusted for trophies, plaques, and custom engraving across four generations of teams, businesses and families.

We're a small, family-run shop by design. The person who takes your order is the person who engraves it — on the same machines, under the same roof at 332 N Milwaukee Avenue. That's how it's been since 1949, and it's why the work still looks right.

Sports leagues, corporations, schools, service clubs, or a single heartfelt gift — if it can be engraved, we'd love to make it for you.
